Earth on a marker

A rotating Earth appears on top of the Hiro marker when your camera sees it.

Same Hiro marker as the other demos. Have it ready, then tap Start camera.

  1. Open the marker on another screen, or print it.
  2. Tap Start camera and allow camera access.
  3. Point your camera at the marker, about 30 cm away.
