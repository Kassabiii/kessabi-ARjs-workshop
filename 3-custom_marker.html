Object switcher

Pick Earth, Saturn or a shape, and it appears on top of the Hiro marker.

  1. Open the marker on another screen, or print it.
  2. Tap Start camera and allow camera access.
  3. Point your camera at the marker, then use the buttons at the bottom to switch objects.
